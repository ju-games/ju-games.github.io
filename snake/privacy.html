Privacy Policy for Snake

JU Games · Effective 29 September 2026

Snake does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for, collect or send us any information about you or your device. We cannot identify you, and we have no servers that receive data from the app.

What stays on your device

To remember your choices, the game saves these settings on your device only:

  • Your selected speed level
  • Your selected maze
  • Your top score

These never leave your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Snake does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal information. It does not need an internet connection to play.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or social media SDKs. Snake is built with the Unity game engine, and all optional Unity services are turned off. We do not receive or have access to any data through Unity.

Children

Snake is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/ju-games/ju-games.github.io/issues.
