Privacy Policy for Glowstep

JU Games · Effective 4 October 2026

Glowstep does not collect personal data, and we (JU Games) receive none. The game is free and shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob. Google handles the ads and the information they need, as described below. There are no accounts, analytics, chat or in-app purchases.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for, collect or send us any information about you or your device. We cannot identify you, and we have no servers that receive data from the game. The ads described below are provided by Google, not by us.

What stays on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file on your device only. It contains:

  • Your stars and best score for each level, your Echo Run best and your best Daily Trial result
  • Your gems, the critters you have unlocked and the one you are using
  • Your daily gift streak, today's mission progress and the date you last claimed a reward
  • Your recent level results, used only on your device to gently slow the glow if a level is giving you trouble
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration, calm mode and whether ads are switched off on this device

This file never leaves your device and is not linked to your name or any account. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. The Daily Trial and daily gift use your device's clock to know the date; no internet connection is used for this.

Ads

To keep the game free, Glowstep shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • a small banner at the bottom of the home and results screens (never while you are playing), and
  • an optional video you can choose to watch for an extra heart when you run out. Watching it is never required.

Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating. To deliver ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes information such as the device type and operating system, app version, an approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped) and device identifiers. On Android this includes the device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). On iOS, Glowstep never asks to track you through App Tracking Transparency, so Google does not receive the iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Depending on your choices, Google may use this information to show ads that are more relevant to you. This processing is done by Google under its own privacy policy; JU Games does not receive this information. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Tapping an ad may open a web page or app store outside the game.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in a region where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy choices in the game.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can opt out of personalised ads or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. You can also manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • No data with us: because JU Games holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your device.

Permissions

Glowstep does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal information. It does not need an internet connection to play; ads are only shown when the device is online.

On Android, the game lists a few standard permissions that are granted automatically and never shown as a prompt:

  • Vibration: a short buzz when you step on a wrong tile. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
  • Network access: used only to load the ads described above.
  • Advertising ID (and the related Android ad-services permissions): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

Third parties

The only third-party SDK in the game is the Google Mobile Ads SDK, including Google's User Messaging Platform for consent (see Ads). There are no analytics or social media SDKs. Glowstep is built with the Unity game engine, and all optional Unity services are turned off. We do not receive or have access to any data through Unity. The game's font, Fredoka, is bundled inside the app; nothing is downloaded while you play.

Children

Glowstep is rated for everyone, but it is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game itself collects none from anyone. The game has no purchases, accounts, or ways to contact other players. If you are a parent and have questions, please contact us using the link below.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change what data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before releasing that version, and the app store listings will be updated to match.
