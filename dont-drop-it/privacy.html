Privacy Policy for Don't Drop It!

JU Games · Effective 2 October 2026

Don't Drop It! does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, chat or in-app purchases.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for, collect or send us any information about you or your device. We cannot identify you, and we have no servers that receive data from the game.

What stays on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file on your device only. It contains:

  • Your best score and your best Daily Challenge score
  • Your gems, the skins you have unlocked and the skin you are using
  • Your daily gift streak, today's mission progress and the date you last claimed a reward
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration and calm mode

This file never leaves your device and is not linked to your name or any account. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. The Daily Challenge and daily gift use your device's clock to know the date; no internet connection is used for this.

Permissions

Don't Drop It! does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal information, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

On Android, the game may list two standard permissions that are granted automatically and never shown as a prompt:

  • Vibration: a short buzz when the ball falls. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
  • Network access: added automatically by the Unity engine. The game does not use it to send any data.

Third parties

The game contains no advertising, analytics or social media SDKs. Don't Drop It! is built with the Unity game engine, and all optional Unity services are turned off. We do not receive or have access to any data through Unity. The game's font, Fredoka, is bundled inside the app; nothing is downloaded while you play.

Children

Don't Drop It! is designed to be fun and safe for players of all ages, including children. Because the game collects no data, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country), and the game contains no ads, purchases, links to other apps or ways to contact other players.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change what data is handled (for example, advertising), we will update this page and the effective date above before releasing that version, and the app store listings will be updated to match.
