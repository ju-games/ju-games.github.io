Privacy Policy for Don't Drop It!

JU Games · Effective 3 October 2026

Don't Drop It! does not collect personal data, and we (JU Games) receive none. The game is free and shows a small number of family-safe ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalised ads and no tracking. There are no accounts, analytics, chat or in-app purchases.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for, collect or send us any information about you or your device. We cannot identify you, and we have no servers that receive data from the game. The ads described below are provided by Google, not by us.

What stays on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file on your device only. It contains:

  • Your best score and your best Daily Challenge score
  • Your gems, the skins you have unlocked and the skin you are using
  • Your daily gift streak, today's mission progress and the date you last claimed a reward
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration and calm mode

This file never leaves your device and is not linked to your name or any account. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. The Daily Challenge and daily gift use your device's clock to know the date; no internet connection is used for this.

Ads

To keep the game free, Don't Drop It! shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • a small banner at the bottom of the home and results screens (never while you are playing),
  • an occasional full-screen ad between games, and
  • an optional video you can choose to watch to keep going after the ball falls. Watching it is never required.

Every ad request is marked as directed to children (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA, and equivalent rules such as the GDPR), with the strictest content rating ("G"). This means Google shows only non-personalised, family-appropriate ads, does not use the device's advertising ID for personalised advertising or remarketing, and does not build a profile of the player.

To deliver ads, measure how they are shown and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes limited technical information such as the device type and operating system, app version, an approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interactions (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped). This processing is done by Google under its own privacy policy; JU Games does not receive this information. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's policies for child-directed apps.

Tapping an ad may open a web page or app store outside the game.

Permissions

Don't Drop It! does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal information. It does not need an internet connection to play; ads are only shown when the device is online.

On Android, the game may list two standard permissions that are granted automatically and never shown as a prompt:

  • Vibration: a short buzz when the ball falls. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
  • Network access: used only to load the ads described above.

Third parties

The only third-party SDK in the game is the Google Mobile Ads SDK (see Ads). There are no analytics or social media SDKs. Don't Drop It! is built with the Unity game engine, and all optional Unity services are turned off. We do not receive or have access to any data through Unity. The game's font, Fredoka, is bundled inside the app; nothing is downloaded while you play.

Children

Don't Drop It! is designed to be fun and safe for players of all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Ads are configured as child-directed and non-personalised, as described above. The game has no purchases, accounts, or ways to contact other players.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change what data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before releasing that version, and the app store listings will be updated to match.
